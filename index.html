GOOD TO KNOW

A few things
you might ask.

Quick answers to help you shop with confidence.

How do I place an order? +

Choose a product, open its details, select your size and add it to your cart. Enter your delivery information in the cart and place the Cash on Delivery order.

Which payment method is available? +

This storefront is set up for Cash on Delivery. Pay for your order when it arrives. Online payment is not enabled in this demo.

How do I choose the right size? +

Open a product’s detail view to see the available size options. Size availability depends on the product and the store catalogue.

Can I save products for later? +

Yes. Tap the heart on a product to add it to your wishlist. Your wishlist is saved in this browser on this device.

Can I change or cancel my order? +

Please use the Contact page to prepare an enquiry. The seller will need to confirm any changes or cancellation.
